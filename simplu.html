People, Faith & History (DERTOUR & Wikipedia)

Apostolic Christianity & Cultural Coexistence

Introduced in 45 AD by Apostle Paul, the autocephalous Church of Cyprus is one of the oldest independent Orthodox churches in the world—structured into an Archbishopric in Nicosia, 5 metropolises, and 11 monasteries. Under Archbishop Makarios III (1959), Cyprus became the only state alongside the Vatican led by a prelate.

Orthodox Christianity (South majority) 72.3% – 80%
Sunni Islam (North • strong secular "laik" tradition) 18% – 25%
Minorities: Armenian, Latin & Kormakiti Maronite Arabic ~2.7% (~25k)
Historic Milestone: June 4–6, 2010 marked the first-ever Papal Apostolic Visit to Cyprus by Pope Benedict XVI at Eleftheria Stadium in Nicosia.
